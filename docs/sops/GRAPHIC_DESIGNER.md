# Graphic Designer Quality SOP

## Review objective

Confirm that a design satisfies the brief, audience, medium, brand system, and
call to action while remaining readable, original, and technically suitable
for its delivery platform.

## Required checks

- Understand the task purpose, audience, medium, dimensions, and platform.
- Select colors from the approved brand/logo palette and preserve contrast.
- Confirm correct logo, capitalization, typography, hierarchy, readability,
  spacing, alignment, contact details, and call to action.
- Check source quality and licensing; reject watermarked, low-resolution, or
  plagiarized material.
- Proofread the final exported artifact as well as the editable source.

## Mistake taxonomy for assessment content

- spelling, capitalization, punctuation, quotation-mark, and proofreading
  errors;
- text alignment, inconsistent fonts, poor typography, insufficient hierarchy,
  readability, or spacing;
- incorrect text color, weak contrast, overlap, clutter, or poor use of
  negative space;
- watermarked, low-resolution, improperly licensed, or copied imagery;
- incorrect medium, dimensions, platform treatment, or export;
- absent/incorrect logo, contact information, or call to action;
- unnecessary borders and decoration; and
- content or visual treatment unsuited to the intended audience.

Review evidence should capture an image region when applicable, severity,
expected correction, and the governing quality criterion.
