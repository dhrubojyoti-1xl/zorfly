# Video Editor Quality SOP

## Review objective

Confirm that a video communicates the assigned purpose, follows the brand,
uses suitable high-quality footage, and remains clear, consistent, legally
usable, and technically clean.

## Required checks

- Verify task purpose, target audience, brief, format, and delivery platform.
- Confirm correct logo visibility, approved logo/color treatment, tagline,
  capitalization, typography, subtitles, and call to action.
- Review style consistency, pacing, transitions, footage quality, color
  treatment, background, spacing, contrast, and readability.
- Confirm licensed or original media and identify copyright risk.
- Check the final export at normal speed and at relevant frame boundaries.

## Mistake taxonomy for assessment content

- spelling, repeated words, capitalization, and incorrect on-screen text;
- logo, tagline, subtitle, or text-color errors;
- opacity/transparency, color grading, green-screen spill, and contrast errors;
- flicker, rough edges, poor masking, low-quality footage, or visual artifacts;
- spacing, alignment, timing, pacing, transition, and consistency errors;
- missing or incorrect call to action; and
- copyright, licensing, or provenance violations.

Reviewers should identify the mistake, location or timestamp, severity,
expected correction, and supporting SOP criterion.
