# Content Writing Quality SOP

## Review objective

Produce accurate, audience-appropriate, original writing that follows the
brief, research evidence, brand voice, formatting standard, and applicable
content-safety restrictions.

## Required checks

- Understand the audience, objective, channel, content type, and call to
  action before drafting.
- Research from reliable sources and preserve references or permissions.
- Follow the approved voice, capitalization, headings, formatting, promotion
  rules, and brand values.
- Avoid prohibited or inappropriate material, including adult content, hate,
  abuse, unsupported rumours, confidential information, inflammatory
  political/war/crime content, and content hostile to applicable national or
  organizational values.
- Proofread for language, factual accuracy, consistency, links, originality,
  and presentation. Avoid unsupported price-comparison or “low price” claims.

## Mistake taxonomy for assessment content

- font/formatting, headings, line spacing, justification, bullets, and broken
  hyperlink errors;
- spelling, capitalization, grammar, tense, agreement, article, preposition,
  and pronoun errors;
- punctuation, sentence-structure, clarity, wordiness, repetition, and poor
  sentence-variety errors;
- tone, audience, purpose, brand, or call-to-action mismatch;
- factual, citation, plagiarism, confidentiality, or prohibited-content risk;
  and
- incomplete proofreading or inconsistent formatting.

Reviewers should record the affected text range, error category, severity,
suggested correction, rationale, and any source or SOP criterion used.
